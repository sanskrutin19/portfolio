      01Flagship · Internship · 2026

      Private AI · RAG · Document Intelligence

      Answers you can actually verify.

      Offline RAG platform for civil tender documents

      Problem

      Tender documents run to hundreds of pages, and one missed clause on eligibility, EMD or penalties can sink a bid. A chatbot that sounds confident without evidence is worse than useless. The documents are also confidential.

      What I built

      A fully offline RAG platform that answers eligibility, EMD, scope, risk and penalty questions with the exact source sentence and page number. It runs on Ollama, ChromaDB and SQLite, and no document or question leaves the machine.

      • Python
      • FastAPI
      • Ollama
      • ChromaDB
      • SQLite FTS5
      • Cross-encoder reranking
      • MCP

      Architecture · click a stage, or run a query

      Ingest

      Tender documents are loaded and processed on the local machine. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party API.

      Illustrative demo · fictional tender text, not product output

      QWhat is the EMD and how must it be submitted?

      sample_tender.pdf · page 7 · clause 4.2

      4.2 Earnest Money Deposit. The bidder shall furnish an Earnest Money Deposit of ₹2,50,000 by bank guarantee or online transfer along with the technical bid. The EMD of unsuccessful bidders shall be returned after award of contract. Bids not accompanied by the EMD shall be summarily rejected.

      A

      ₹2,50,000, by bank guarantee or online transfer, submitted with the technical bid. Bids without it are rejected.

      ❝ The bidder shall furnish an Earnest Money Deposit of ₹2,50,000… ❞ sample_tender.pdf · p.7 · cl. 4.2

      Decision · hybrid retrieval

      Embeddings catch paraphrases, and keyword search catches exact clause numbers, amounts and defined terms that embeddings blur. I run both, fuse them by rank, then let a cross-encoder reranker read each query–passage pair before anything reaches the model.

      Decision · sentence-level evidence

      Citing a whole chunk is not enough to verify an answer. Sentence-level span selection picks the specific sentence that supports the answer, so the citation is verbatim and comes with a page number.

      Decision · measure it

      I measured retrieval against a golden question set on recall, rank and latency, so changes to chunking or reranking could be compared instead of eyeballed.

      Modules built on the retrieval layer

      • Go / No-Go
      • Risk finder
      • Synopsis
      • Pre-bid queries
      • Contradiction finder
      • Redmine via MCP server

      What I built

      • Architected the platform with OMEMO2 (Signal Protocol) Double Ratchet encryption across messages, media and calls.
      • A local-first storage layer (IndexedDB plus an encrypted key store) for offline history and media caching, so the app stays fully usable without a persistent connection.
      • WebRTC voice and video through coturn TURN/STUN, and encrypted file sharing up to 500 MB via chunked AES-GCM streaming.
      • Android and iOS builds from one React codebase with Capacitor. The full stack runs in Docker Compose.
